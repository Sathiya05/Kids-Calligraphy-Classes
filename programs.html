Stage at a glance

Which writing stage?

4–6 · Foundation Writing

Building grip, strokes and first letters.

7–9 · Handwriting Improvement

Formation, spacing and alignment habits.

10–12 · Advanced Handwriting

Neatness, consistency and page presentation.

13+ · Calligraphy & Creative

Styling, pen control and expression.

Stages are described as guidance — placement is always confirmed by an instructor after assessment.
